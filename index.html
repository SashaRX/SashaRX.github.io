Atmosphere, light & shadows

Rayleigh scattering creates the blue sky and atmospheric rim; Mie scattering adds the forward glow around the sun. Ozone absorption and multiple scattering shape the colour and light in the atmosphere.

Change the sun direction, colour temperature and atmospheric density live. Planetary eclipses, object shadows and sun shafts reveal how light travels through air and fog.

Froxel volumetric fog

A froxel is a cell in a 3D grid fitted to the camera's view. The renderer evaluates scattering and light transmission in this volume, integrates slices along the view and reconstructs fog for the final image, rather than repeating a full fog march for every screen pixel.

Height profiles, drifting density banks and object shadows give the fog depth and movement. Grid resolution trades fine detail for speed. Planets → Fog controls density, wind and colour; the spatial denoise options in Camera → Fog quality apply to the alternative march path, not the default froxel volume.

AA: MSAA, SMAA, CMAA2 & TAA

MSAA smooths geometry edges during scene rendering. SMAA detects and blends edges in the rendered image. CMAA2 offers another edge-aware filter, with strength and sharpness controls; on WebGL2 it falls back to SMAA.

This demo's TAA combines SMAA with a bounded two-frame temporal filter, using depth and neighbourhood clamping to limit history. It can calm shimmer, with a softness or trailing trade-off during motion. Compare modes in Camera → AA mode; scene MSAA can also run under post-AA.

MipMap bloom

The HDR image is downsampled into a pyramid of smaller images, then upsampled and blended. Fine levels keep local highlights; coarse levels spread wider halos, giving the sun and bright atmospheric edges a soft glow.

Look → Bloom exposes intensity, curve and falloff. Blur mix shapes the MIP pyramid used by the bloom viewer and fog light bleed, rather than the tonemap glow. Fog bleed samples the previous frame's pyramid and affects sky light, keeping opaque silhouettes crisp.

HDR, tonemapping & image quality

Exposure and Reinhard, ACES, Neutral or AgX tonemapping bring HDR lighting into the displayed range. Output dithering reduces visible bands in smooth gradients; CAS/RCAS sharpening adjusts the final image's crispness.

Reduced-resolution atmosphere rendering offers another quality/speed trade-off. Use Camera for these controls, Look for sun and bloom, and Planets for atmosphere and fog.
